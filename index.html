CASE STUDIES

Two projects, in detail

A finance platform's Payment Extension, end to end

The brief was bigger than a bug fix: connect a finance-sector merchant's own payment platform to Shopify checkout through a fully custom Payment Extension authorization, transaction status, the works, not just a themed checkout button.

While building that integration, the existing approach was exposing API tokens in frontend requests, with payment data protection that didn't hold up to what a finance client needs.

Tokens sent straight from the browser → Tokens verified and held server-side

Result: a working, PCI-conscious Payment Extension linking the merchant's finance platform to Shopify checkout, with the token-exposure gap closed as part of the build not bolted on after.

Loyalty points, built into checkout

A merchant wanted customers to earn and redeem loyalty points without leaving checkout. I designed and built a Shopify app around a third-party Stamp Loyalty API, so points issuance and redemption happen natively at checkout instead of through a separate portal.
